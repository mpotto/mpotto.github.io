I am a PhD student in the Department of Statistics at the University of
Washington, advised by [Marco Carone](https://www.biostat.washington.edu/people/marco-carone) and
[Andrea Rotnitzky](https://www.biostat.washington.edu/people/andrea-rotnitzky).
My research interests lie broadly in nonparametric and semiparametric statistical methods for public health data. My current work focuses on adaptivity to smoothness in shape-constrained inference.

Before coming to Seattle, I completed an M.Sc. in Statistics in the joint
program of the University of São Paulo and the Federal University of São
Carlos, advised by
[Rafael Izbicki](https://rafaelizbicki.com), and a B.Sc. in Computational Physics at the University of São Paulo.
